\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This manuscript is a design and analysis artifact, not a completed empirical paper. The largest limitation is the absence of measured phone results. The conceptual plots and empty tables cannot support superiority, runtime, or energy claims and must be replaced.

The trusted computing base is substantial. A kernel, privileged service, model loader, or training runtime compromise defeats complete mediation. Application-only deployment can protect its own pipeline but cannot establish all cross-application provenance claims.

Origin is not intent. User-authored text can be socially engineered, copied, dictated, or generated by an accessibility service. Shared devices and account delegation complicate the authorship bit. PoisonGuard records uncertainty and evaluates induced retyping, but does not solve human authentication or manipulation.

Exact replay depends on retention and determinism. Mobile accelerators may use nondeterministic kernels; vendors may remove old runtimes; privacy policy may require payload deletion; and base-model licenses may constrain archival. When these conditions fail, PoisonGuard can reset or approximately mitigate, but the exact-restoration theorem no longer applies.

Lineage completeness is difficult to validate for opaque transformations. A summary may depend on hidden context, cached state, or system prompts not represented as explicit parents. The prototype must instrument those dependencies or narrow its claim. The proof cannot repair missing edges.

Behavioral and spectral audits are not universal detectors. Adaptive attacks can match clean norms or avoid known probes, while benign non-IID personal data can appear anomalous. The influence bound in \cref{prop:influence} does not imply a small ASR. False quarantine may disproportionately affect unusual but legitimate users.

The planned datasets approximate private phone streams. PersonaChat, AESLC, and LaMP do not fully represent multilingual messaging, images, code-switching, accessibility input, or calendar semantics. Real-device integration may require privileged APIs unavailable to third-party apps. Results must distinguish synthetic harnesses from production feasibility.

Finally, exact rollback removes identified causal descendants but does not discover unidentified poison. A poisoned and a clean event can also induce similar behavior. Recovery certificates attest to execution over a declared revocation set; they are not global certificates of model safety.
